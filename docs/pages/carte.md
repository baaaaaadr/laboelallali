# Page: /carte — the lab's business card

## Purpose
The business card in the app (Dr Aziz, 12/10/2026, devis line 63): save the lab in one's phone contacts, download the card, every detail clickable. Reached from « Scannez-moi » (`/qr`, tile « Carte de visite ») and the contact page (`#carte`).

## Directory & File
- **Route:** `src/app/[lang]/carte/page.tsx` (client) → `BusinessCard`; `layout.tsx` (server): `carte.meta_title` / `meta_description` (indexable).
- **Component:** `src/components/features/qr/BusinessCard.tsx`.
- **Files** (`public/carte-visite/`): `carte-recto.png`, `carte-verso.png`, `carte-visite-laboratoire-el-allali.pdf` — COPIES of the print work's digital card (`marketing/output/imprimerie/carte-visite-numerique-{recto,verso}.png`, `carte-visite-numerique.pdf`): one source for screen and printer; re-copy them when the printed card changes. `laboratoire-el-allali.vcf` — generated by `node scripts/generate-vcard.js` (vCard 3.0, CRLF, folded at 75 octets; three numbers with iPhone labels `X-ABLabel`, address, e-mail, site, hours in NOTE, the app icon as PHOTO).

## Context & Key Components
- The card (recto, verso), then « Enregistrer dans mes contacts » (`carte-vcf`, `download`), « Image » (`carte-png`) and « PDF » (`carte-pdf`).
- Clickable rows (`data-row`): landline, lab WhatsApp, Dr El Allali WhatsApp + call (0661291411), e-mail, site, address → Google Maps; hours from `monday_to_friday` / `saturday_hours` (Saturday until 13h00) + « Dimanche fermé ». Phone numbers `dir="ltr"`.
- `GoogleSignInPrompt` hidden on `carte`.

## Notes for AI
- A number, the address or the hours change → `src/constants/contact.ts`, `src/constants/labHours.ts`, the locales, AND re-run `scripts/generate-vcard.js` (the .vcf is a static file), AND the printed card.
- Bench: `npm run test:qr:ui` (K1, K2).
